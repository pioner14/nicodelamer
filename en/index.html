About

Nico de la Mer is a music producer and beatmaker based in Saint-Nazaire, France. He also works on mixing, arrangement and music for media.

Blending organic acoustic guitars, Parisian accordion nuances, analog Rhodes warmth, and soothing Atlantic field recordings, his music crafts a tranquil Balearic sanctuary.

Recorded and produced in his seaside studio in Saint-Nazaire, each piece is an invitation to pause, breathe, and experience the quiet beauty of the Atlantic horizon.

  • French Balearic Chillout
  • Downtempo
  • Ocean Ambient
